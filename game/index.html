PyLadies 是國際性的社群，希望幫助更多女性成為 Python 開源社群的參與者與領導者。

PyLadies is an international community helping more women become active participants and leaders in the Python open-source community.

全世界已經有超過 145 個 Chapter，有些以城市命名，有些以國家命名。每個 Chapter 都由當地夥伴獨立運作，各自舉辦活動、發展自己的特色，連 Logo 都長得不一樣！

There are more than 145 chapters around the world — some named after cities, some after countries. Each one is run independently by local organizers, with its own events, its own style, and even its own logo!
